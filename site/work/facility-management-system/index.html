      Facility Management

      SaudiPak Facility Management System, Dashboard UX and Full-Stack

      ProductSaudiPak facility platform
      IndustryProperty and facilities
      Year2023
      PlatformWeb
      Delivered asTeam lead and full-stack developer, NXCSOL

      Overview

      A facility-management platform for the SaudiPak tower: complaints, job slips, tenants and occupancy, plant-room logs, janitorial duty, security and CCTV, with role-based access for 260+ users across 6 roles.

      The challenge

      Running a commercial tower meant paper job slips, WhatsApp complaints and plant-room logs on clipboards. Management had no single picture of what was open, who was on duty or how the building was performing.

      problem / solution

      What we set out to fix

      Problem

      • Tenant complaints arrived by phone and chat, with no tracking.
      • Job slips, duty rosters and plant logs were on paper.
      • Six kinds of staff needed different access to the same data.
      260+users on the platform
      6roles with their own access
      15+pool cars in fleet booking

      Solution

      • Complaints and job slips tracked from first response to resolution.
      • Hourly plant-room logs and daily janitorial rosters.
      • Tenants and occupancy by floor and lease.
      • Role-based access and productivity dashboards.

      stack.md

      The tech stack, and why I used it

      Next.js and ReactDashboards and long data-entry forms for six different roles in one app.
      Node.js with real-time APIsComplaints and job slips update for everyone as they change.
      MongoDBPlant logs, duty rosters and complaints all have different shapes, and a document database handles that without constant schema changes.
      Tailwind CSSA calm, consistent interface for people who use it all day.
