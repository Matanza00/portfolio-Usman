      Karighar

      Karighar Home-Services App UI/UX Redesign

      ProductHome-services booking app
      IndustryHome services
      Year2026
      PlatformiOS and Android
      Delivered asUI/UX design and front-end

      Overview

      Karighar connects Karachi households with verified plumbers, electricians and AC technicians. The redesign turns booking a home-service pro into a calm, three-tap flow: pick a service, watch your pro arrive on a live map, and pay cash on completion.

      The challenge

      The app had to earn trust from people letting a stranger into their home, work for cash-first customers, and stay simple on budget Android phones, all without hiding the details people need before they confirm a job.

      problem / solution

      What we set out to fix

      Problem

      • No way to tell a vetted pro from an unknown one before booking.
      • After booking, customers were left guessing when the pro would arrive.
      • Payment and pricing only became clear at the door.
      8core screens, from booking to rating
      2full themes, light and dark
      1primary action per screen

      Solution

      • CNIC-verified pros with ratings and job counts on every card.
      • Ride-app style live tracking from confirmation to arrival.
      • A cash-first checkout with the full breakdown before you confirm.
      • One saffron action per screen, so the next step is never in doubt.

      process

      How we built it

      01

      Research

      Studied the live Karighar beta and the booking, tracking and payment patterns people already trust.

      02

      Flows

      Mapped one path: book, track, pay and rate, with nothing in between.

      03

      Visual system

      A sage ground, white cards, forest identity and a single saffron action colour.

      04

      Prototype

      A clickable 390×844 prototype with complete light and dark themes.

      features

      What it does

      feature 01

      Book in three taps

      • Browse services with live pricing, from plumbing and electrical to AC repair.
      • Every pro shows CNIC verification, rating and completed jobs.

      feature 02

      See them coming

      • A live map of nearby pros, ranked by distance and rating.
      • Once confirmed, track your pro's arrival in real time.

      feature 03

      Pay, rate, done

      • A cash-first checkout with a clear breakdown before you confirm.
      • Rate the pro and the job, then find it again in your bookings.
