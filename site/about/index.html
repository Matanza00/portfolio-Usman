      ~/about · senior full-stack developer

      Usman Nadeem, full-stack.

      I'm Usman Nadeem, a senior full-stack developer in Kalispell, Montana, with five years building scalable SaaS platforms and enterprise systems. I work across the stack: React and Next.js interfaces, Node, Express and NestJS APIs, and PostgreSQL, MySQL or MongoDB behind them.

      At Legit Design Studio I've shipped production products including Obyect.io, Subyect and the iFund app, building role-based dashboards and AI-assisted analytics, often two or more weeks ahead of deadline. Before that I delivered SaaS at Nexifies and led the frontend of a national fleet-management system at SOS Globals that tracked 580+ vehicles across 12+ cities. I also spent a year at United Bank Limited (UBL) as a contract Senior Automation Engineer and Senior Software Engineer, taking banking projects such as Remittance, NPC, ADC, Vault Management and IBM BPM from design through testing, UAT and deployment.

      I hold a BS in Computer Science from NUCES-FAST and I'm certified across Docker, AWS and Google Cloud. I care about the details that make software dependable: fast dashboards, accessible ADA/WCAG-compliant interfaces, and workflows that make sense the first time.
